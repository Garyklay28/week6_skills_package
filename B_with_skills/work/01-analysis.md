## Content Summary

Short coming-of-age / suspense film. Two kids play hide-and-seek by an abandoned
railway tunnel. Kid A (the "believer") says an old man came out of the tunnel and
warned that a train is coming. Kid B (the "skeptic", the teaser) mocks this: the line
is overgrown, no train has come in years. They tease each other (scaredy-cat, a lucky
charm thrown away, a fake "train's coming!" prank). In the last scene the roles flip:
B gets stuck on the tracks, A can't pull him free, and a real train arrives.

The script is a dialogue list only: no speaker tags, no timecodes, many one- or
two-character interjections. Translation must stay line-aligned (97 -> 97).

## Tone & Style

- Register: northern Mandarin kids' colloquial speech. Markers: 儿化 (小孩儿), 啥 (54, 84),
  咱 (16), 整我 (67, "mess with me"), 至于吗 (69, "is that really a big deal"),
  一惊一乍 (38, "jumpy, freaking out over nothing"), 呗 / 嘛 / 喽 / 啦 / 呀 sentence particles,
  区区一个破隧道 (52, mock-grand "a mere crummy tunnel").
- Teasing, bragging, dares. Kids sound like kids: contractions, short bursts, "gonna",
  "quit it", no adult hedging.
- Suspense is carried by plain lines ("看呐真有火车来了", "火车来了 拽不动你", "快跑"):
  keep them flat and short so the fear lands.
- English code-switch "OK?" (23) in the source: keep "OK?" as a smug tag.

## Translation Challenges

- **4 小孩儿 / 5 哎**: ambiguous speaker (old man vs. B) -> keep "Hey, kid." which works for either; 哎 as a neutral reply.
- **6 被我抓傻了**: literally "caught stupid by me" -> idiomatic teasing ("Did I scare you stiff?" / "Did getting caught fry your brain?").
- **36 你试试**: after A says "no", B presses: "Just try it." (not a threat here).
- **38 一惊一乍**: idiom, "jumpy over nothing" -> "Quit freaking out every two seconds."
- **41 / 56 砰**: onomatopoeia. 41 = A popping out to startle B (finger-gun "Bang!"); 56 = sound/word as B flings the charm. Glossary: "Bang!" both times (playful mouth-sound, consistent).
- **42 / 85 嘿嘿 / 哎嘿**: giggle / taunting "Heh-heh" -> "Hehe." / "Ha!".
- **52 区区一个破隧道**: mock-formal 区区 + crude 破 -> "It's just some crummy old tunnel."
- **55 护身符啊**: mocking discovery, "A lucky charm? Seriously?"
- **69 至于吗**: "Is it that serious?" -> "It was a joke. Relax."
- **72 下次赢了就是你赢了**: B's "generous" rule: win next round and you're the winner -> "Win next round and it's yours."
- **74 / 1 countdowns**: numbers only; keep digits, ASCII commas.
- **81-82 抓**: hide-and-seek "catch/tag". Use "catch" consistently.
- **89 你也有今天呀**: schadenfreude idiom, "you finally get yours" -> "Look who's stuck now!"
- **93 我可抓住你了啊**: double meaning: game "caught you" + physically gripping A's arm as the train comes. Keep "Got you now!" ambiguity.
- **95 拽不动你**: "I can't pull you free" (not "drag").
- **Interjections (13 啊？, 20 哎呀, 47 喂, 88 啊, 94 哎呀)**: render as English interjections (Huh? / Ugh. / Hey! / Ah! / Argh!) so no line is empty.
